How to Identify a “Hot Mode” in CMB Slots

A slot is likely entering a high-activity cycle when you notice:

1. Special symbols appear more often - (Thor, treasure chest, queens, vampire marks)

2. Cascades / combos trigger frequently - (Gummy Bonanza is especially sensitive)

3. Many small wins appear back-to-back - Indicating approaching Bonus or Free Spins.
